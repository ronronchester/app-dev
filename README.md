## About the Series

**Bleach** is a Japanese manga written and illustrated by *Tite Kubo*. It ran in *Weekly Shonen Jump* from 2001 to 2016 and was adapted into an anime by Studio Pierrot. It follows **Ichigo Kurosaki**, a high school student who becomes a **Soul Reaper**, a guardian who fights evil spirits called **Hollows** and guides souls to the afterlife, the **Soul Society**.

## Why I Like It

1. The sword fights and special abilities are stylish and exciting.
2. The soundtrack and character designs are memorable.
3. The story has strong themes of protecting friends and growing stronger.
